# Green Vizag – Smart Climate Action & Waste Management Platform (Track 03)

## Aligned Features
- **Smart Waste Segregation Scanner**: Classify waste into Organic, Recyclable, or E-Waste categories.
- **Carbon Footprint Tracker**: Log daily eco-actions to compute personal and citywide CO₂ offset.
- **Tree-Planting & Urban Cover Map**: Interactive map displaying urban canopy cover and heat island zones across Visakhapatnam.
- **Garbage Reporting & Tracking System**: Report garbage hotspots and track resolution progress.
